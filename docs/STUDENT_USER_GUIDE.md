# Board of Science and Technical Education (BSTE) Islamabad
## Candidate & Public Result Verification User Guide

**Target Audience:** Students, Graduates, Employers, Academic Institutions, and Evaluation Agencies  
**Public Website URL:** `https://bste.edu.pk` or `http://localhost:3000`  

---

## 1. How to Verify Your Examination Result Online

1. Visit the official BSTE Islamabad portal at `https://bste.edu.pk` or navigate to `/verify`.
2. Locate the **"Search Examination Result"** box.
3. Enter your assigned Roll Number (Format: `BSTE-YYYY-XXXXX`, e.g., `BSTE-2026-00125`).
4. Click the **"Search Result"** button.

---

## 2. Reading Your Verified Result Card

Once verified, your official **BSTE Electronic Result Card** will appear:
- **Student Profile:** Your verified photograph, Full Name, Father's Name, Roll Number, Registration Number, and Masked CNIC (`61101-*******-3`).
- **Academic Credentials:** Name of your affiliated polytechnic college, academic department, program title (e.g. Diploma of Associate Engineer in Computer Information Technology), and passing session.
- **Detailed Marks Transcript:** Row-by-row breakdown of all enrolled subjects with individual Theory Marks, Practical Marks, Total Marks, and Subject Letter Grade.
- **Final Result Summary:** Total Obtained Marks, Maximum Marks, Aggregate Percentage, Overall Grade (`A+`, `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `F`), and Final Outcome (`PASSED`, `FAILED`, `DISTINCTION`).
- **Digital Security Seal:** Authorized signature of the Controller of Examinations and high-density cryptographic verification QR code.

---

## 3. Downloading and Printing Your Transcript

- **Download PDF Result Card:** Click the **"Download Result Card"** button at the top right to download a high-resolution, print-ready PDF of your official transcript.
- **Print Direct:** Click the **"Print Transcript"** button to print your result card directly on A4 paper formatted for official document submission.

---

## 4. Cryptographic QR Code Verification for Employers

Every verified result contains a unique **2D QR Code**:
1. Scan the QR code using any smartphone camera or QR scanner.
2. The scanner will open the authentic BSTE validation link: `https://bste.edu.pk/verify/{certificate-id}`.
3. The page confirms the candidate's authentic credentials directly from the central BSTE database with a green **"Verified Authentic Certificate"** seal, completely preventing forged or altered documents.
